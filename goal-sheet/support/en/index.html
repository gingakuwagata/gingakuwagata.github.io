Where is my data stored?

On your device and, if you have made the one-time purchase and use iCloud, in your own iCloud only. There is no account.

Can I move my charts to a new device?

Yes. With the one-time purchase and iCloud on the same Apple Account, your charts sync to the new device.

What is free?

One chart, writing, moving cells, flower marks, saving and sharing images, and widgets are free. A one-time purchase adds more charts, A4 PDF and printing, iCloud sync, and color themes.

Can I use my purchase on another device?

Yes. On the same Apple Account, use Restore Purchase in Settings.
