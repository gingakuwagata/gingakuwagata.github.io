Goal Setting Sheet Privacy Policy

Yuto Kuwabara (the "Provider") sets out how Goal Setting Sheet (the "App") handles information as follows.

Information the App Handles

  • The App handles the goals and actions you write, cell colors, achievement marks, and chart names and themes. These are stored on your device.
  • To display your chart in widgets, part of its content is stored in an area on your device shared between the App and its widgets. This content is not sent off your device.
  • If you have made the one-time purchase and use iCloud on your device, your chart content is stored in your own iCloud (a private CloudKit database provided by Apple) and synced between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The Provider cannot see this content.
  • When you save an exported image to Photos, the App asks for permission to add photos. The App does not read the contents of your photo library.
  • The App does not require an account and contains no advertising, usage analytics, or crash reporting.
  • To check for required updates, the App reads a settings file (update.json) hosted on GitHub Pages. Only standard web request information is sent; your chart content is never sent.
  • In-app purchases and payments are processed by Apple. The Provider does not receive any payment information.

Purpose of Use and External Services

Your chart content is used only for display, widgets, image and PDF export, and printing. iCloud sync is handled by Apple Inc. and delivery of the settings file by GitHub, Inc., each under their own terms. When you share or print, the data goes to the destination or printer you choose. Except as required by law, the Provider does not provide your information to third parties.

Storage and Deletion

Charts are removed from your device when you delete them in the App or delete the App. Content stored in iCloud can be deleted from Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. The Provider does not keep your charts.

Changes to This Policy

The Provider may change this policy when necessary. The revised policy will be published on this site.
