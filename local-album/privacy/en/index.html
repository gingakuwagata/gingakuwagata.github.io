Local Photo Album Privacy Policy

Yuto Kuwabara (the "Provider") sets out how Local Photo Album (the "App") handles information as follows.

Information the App Handles

  • The App handles the photos and videos you add to it, album names, and the dates you added them. They are stored only in the App's area on your device and are never sent off the device.
  • Stored photos and videos are excluded from device backups (iCloud Backup and backups to a computer), and are protected so they cannot be read while the device is locked. The App does not use iCloud sync.
  • When you add photos and when you put them back into the Photos app, the App uses your Photos library within the access you allow. After adding photos, the App asks whether to delete them from the Photos app, and deleting always goes through the iOS confirmation.
  • The App uses Face ID (or your device passcode) to open. Face data is handled by iOS; neither the App nor the Provider receives it.
  • Export creates a single file encrypted with a password you choose, saved to a location you choose. The password is not stored and the Provider does not know it. If you forget the password, no one can open the exported file.
  • The App does not require an account and includes no advertising, usage analytics, or crash reporting.
  • To check for required updates, the App downloads a settings file (update.json) hosted on GitHub Pages. Only standard web request information is sent; no photos or album contents are sent. This is the only network access the App makes.
  • In-app purchases and payments are processed by Apple. The Provider does not receive payment information.

Purpose of Use and External Services

Photos and videos are used only to display them in the App, to put the ones you choose back into the Photos app, and to export them when you ask. The settings file is delivered by GitHub, Inc. under its own terms. Except as required by law, the Provider does not share your information with third parties.

Storage and Deletion

Photos and videos are removed from the device when you delete them in the App or delete the App. Because they are not included in device backups, export them before deleting the App or changing devices. The Provider does not keep your photos.

Changes to This Policy

The Provider may change this policy when needed. The updated policy will be published on this site.
