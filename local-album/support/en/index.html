Where are my photos stored?

Only in the App's area on this device. They do not appear in the Photos app or in iCloud. There is no account, and your photos are never sent anywhere.

Can I move my photos to a new device?

Photos are not included in device backups. Use Export in Settings to save a single file encrypted with a password, then use Import on the new device.

I forgot my export password

The password is not stored, so no one can open the exported file. If the photos are still in the App, export again with a new password.

What if Face ID is not available?

You can open the App with your device passcode.

What can I do for free?

You can add up to 50 photos and videos in total. A one-time purchase removes the limit.

Can I use my purchase on another device?

With the same Apple Account, use Restore Purchases on the purchase screen.
