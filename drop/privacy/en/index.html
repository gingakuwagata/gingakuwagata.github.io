Drop Privacy Policy

Original

Yuto Kuwabara (hereinafter "Provider") sets out how Drop (hereinafter "App") handles information as follows.

Personal Information

Personal information refers to information about an individual that can identify a specific person through details such as name, date of birth, phone number, email address, or other descriptions contained therein (including information that can be easily combined with other information to identify a specific individual).

Information the App Handles

  • The names of your to-dos, the names, colors and completion times of finished to-dos, and the number of finished to-dos are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the Provider's servers or to any external service such as iCloud.
  • The App has no account registration, sign-in or authentication. You do not need to enter your name or email address.
  • The App contains no analytics or advertising tools (tracking SDKs). It does not collect screen views, usage history or device identifiers.
  • The App connects to the internet only to load the following: a settings file used to check whether an update is required (update.json) and a list of the Provider's other apps (developer-apps.json) from GitHub Pages, and the icons of the listed apps from Apple's servers. When you open another app from the list, an App Store page is loaded inside the App. The App only reads these and does not send your information. When they are loaded, your IP address and similar technical information reach GitHub and Apple as part of how the internet works.
  • When you use "Share This App", an App Store link introducing the App is passed to the destination you choose. Your to-do records are not shared. Review requests use Apple's system feature.

Purposes and External Services

Data on your device is used only to record and show your to-dos. The files and images above are loaded to notify you of updates and to introduce other apps. GitHub, Inc. (file delivery) and Apple Inc. (app icons and App Store pages) each handle information to the extent necessary to provide their services. Except as required by law, the Provider does not provide your information to third parties.

Storage and Deletion

You can delete your to-do records in the App. All data on your device, including the number of finished to-dos, is erased when you delete the App. The Provider does not hold your data. If you have any questions, please use the contact below.

Management and Security of Personal Information

The Provider maintains the accuracy of personal information and manages it securely. The Provider implements appropriate information security measures to prevent the loss, destruction, falsification, or leakage of personal information.

Amendments to This Privacy Policy

The Provider will periodically review the management of personal information, strive for continuous improvement, and may revise this Policy as necessary. Any revised Privacy Policy will be published on this site as needed.

Procedures for Disclosure of Personal Information

The App will respond without delay to requests from individuals regarding notification of the purpose of use, disclosure, correction, addition or deletion of content, suspension of use, erasure, or suspension of third-party provision of their personal information (hereinafter "Disclosure Requests"). For details, please contact us at the address below.
