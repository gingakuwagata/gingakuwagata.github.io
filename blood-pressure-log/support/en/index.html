Where is my data stored?

Your readings are stored only on your device. If you turn on Write to Health in Settings, they are also written to the Health app. There is no account, and iCloud is not used.

Can I move my readings to a new iPhone?

There is no iCloud sync. Restore your new device from a device backup, or write your readings to Health and use Import from Health (available with the one-time purchase) on the new device.

What is free?

Logging, the list, charts, the table and PDF for the last 4 weeks, widgets and controls, and writing to Health are free. A one-time purchase lets you choose any period for the table and PDF and import from Health.

Does the app tell me if my blood pressure is high or low?

No. The app is for recording and viewing only and gives no judgments or health advice. Talk to your doctor about medical decisions.

Can I use my purchase on another device?

Yes, with the same Apple Account. Use Restore Purchases in Settings.
