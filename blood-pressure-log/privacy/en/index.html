Blood Pressure Log Privacy Policy

Yuto Kuwabara (the "Provider") sets out the following policy on the information handled by Blood Pressure Log (the "App").

Information the App Handles

  • The App handles the blood pressure (systolic and diastolic), pulse, date and time, time of day, and notes that you enter. These are stored on your device.
  • To show them in widgets, the latest reading and 7-day averages are stored in an area on your device shared by the App and its widgets. This is not sent off your device.
  • If you turn on "Write to Health" in Settings, your readings are written to Blood Pressure and Heart Rate in the Health app on your device. If you use "Import from Health", the App reads Blood Pressure and Heart Rate from Health for the period you choose and adds them to its readings. Both happen only with your permission. Health data is never sent off your device and is never used for advertising or analytics.
  • The App does not use iCloud. Your readings are kept only on your device and in Health.
  • The App does not require an account and contains no advertising, usage analytics, or crash reporting.
  • To check for required updates, the App loads a settings file (update.json) hosted on GitHub Pages. Only standard web request information is sent; your readings are not.
  • In-app purchases and payments are processed by Apple. The Provider does not receive any payment information.

Purpose of Use and External Services

Your readings are used only for display, charts, creating tables and PDFs, printing, and widgets. The settings file is delivered by GitHub, Inc. under its terms of service. If you share or print a PDF, the data goes to the destination or printer you choose. Except as required by law, the Provider does not provide your information or Health data to third parties.

Storage and Deletion

Readings are removed from your device when you delete them in the App or delete the App. Values written to Health can be deleted in the Health app. The Provider does not keep your readings.

Changes to This Policy

The Provider may change this policy as needed. The revised policy will be published on this site.
