BeatBerry Privacy Policy

Yuto Kuwabara (the "Provider") sets out below how BeatBerry (named "Music Player - Beat Berry" on the U.S. App Store and "Simple Music Player" on some other English-language App Stores; the "App") handles information.

Information the App Handles

  • The music files you import, any track titles you change, your choice of tracks to play for free, and settings such as playback speed and repeat are stored on your device. They are not sent to the Provider's servers.
  • The App has no account registration or sign-in, and does not ask for your name or email address. Version 2.0.0 and later do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Version 1.0.0 uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config to analyze usage and deliver settings, and may send device identifiers, app usage information and approximate location to Google.
  • The title, artist, artwork, playback position and similar details of the track being played are passed to iOS on your device so they can appear on the Lock Screen and in Control Center.

Purposes and External Services

The App downloads a configuration file from GitHub Pages to check for the latest version. This request does not include your music files or track information, but technical information such as your IP address reaches GitHub, Inc. as part of the connection. Apple Inc. processes in-app purchases and restores. If you contact the Provider, what you send is used to respond to you. Except as required by law, your information is not provided to third parties.

Storage and Deletion

You can erase imported tracks and settings from your device by deleting them in the App or by deleting the App. If you use device backups, those backups are governed by the terms of Apple or your backup provider.

Changes to This Policy

The Provider may change this policy as needed. The updated policy will be published on this site.
