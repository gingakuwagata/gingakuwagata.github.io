Stamp Journey Privacy Policy

Yuto Kuwabara (the "Provider") sets out how Stamp Journey (the "App") handles information as follows.

Information the App Handles

  • The App handles the level you set for each prefecture and city, the date and note of each stamp, the stamp design, and the photos you choose. These are stored on your device.
  • If you have made the one-time purchase and use iCloud on your device, your records other than photos (levels for prefectures and cities, stamp dates, notes, and designs) are stored in your own iCloud (a private CloudKit database provided by Apple) and synced between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Photos are never sent to iCloud and are stored only on your device. The Provider cannot see this content.
  • The App reads only the photos you choose from your photo library. Photos are resized and re-encoded when saved, so location and other capture metadata are not kept. When you save a share image to Photos, the App asks for permission to add photos.
  • The App does not require an account and contains no advertising, usage analytics, or crash reporting.
  • To check for required updates, the App reads a settings file (update.json) hosted on GitHub Pages at launch. Only standard web request information is sent; your records are never sent.
  • In-app purchases and payments are processed by Apple. The Provider does not receive any payment information.

Purpose of Use and External Services

Your records and photos are used only to paint the map, show stamps, and create share images and CSV files. iCloud sync is handled by Apple Inc. and delivery of the settings file by GitHub, Inc., each under their own terms. When you share or export, the data goes to the destination you choose. Except as required by law, the Provider does not provide your information to third parties.

Storage and Deletion

Records and photos are removed from your device when you delete them in the App or delete the App. Records stored in iCloud can be deleted from Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. The Provider does not keep your records.

Map Data Sources

  • Created by processing "National Land Numerical Information (Administrative Area Data)" (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, Japan) (https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-N03-2026.html). Boundaries as of January 1, 2026.
  • Romanized and Hangul city names are based on the readings in the "Local Government Codes" (Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications, Japan) (https://www.soumu.go.jp/denshijiti/code.html).

Changes to This Policy

The Provider may change this policy when necessary. The revised policy will be published on this site.
