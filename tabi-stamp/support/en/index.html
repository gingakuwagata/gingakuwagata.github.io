Where is my data stored?

Only on your device and, if you have made the one-time purchase and use iCloud, in your own iCloud. Photos are stored only on your device. There is no account.

Will my records move to a new iPhone?

With the one-time purchase and iCloud on the same Apple Account, your records other than photos sync to your new device. Photos are not synced, so keep the ones you need in your photo library.

What can I use for free?

Painting all 47 prefectures, stamping, up to 3 photos per prefecture, and sharing are free. A one-time purchase unlocks painting cities, unlimited photos, extra stamp designs, and CSV export.

Can I use my purchase on another device?

Yes. With the same Apple ID, tap Restore Purchases in Settings.
