Melt Memo Privacy Policy

Yuto Kuwabara (the "Provider") sets out below how Melt Memo (the "App") handles information.

Information the App Handles

  • Your memo text, its created, updated and deleted times, settings such as the retention period, and your purchase status are stored on your device. They are not sent to the Provider's servers.
  • The App has no account registration and does not ask for your name or email address. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • If you copy a memo, its text is placed on your device's clipboard.

Purposes and External Services

The App downloads a configuration file from GitHub Pages to check for the latest version. This request does not include your memos, but technical information such as your IP address reaches GitHub, Inc. as part of the connection. Apple Inc. processes in-app purchases, restores and refund requests. If you contact the Provider, what you send is used to respond to you. Except as required by law, your information is not provided to third parties.

Storage and Deletion

When the retention period you set has passed, a memo moves to History, and it is deleted from your device one week after that. You can also erase memos by deleting them in the App or by deleting the App. If you use device backups, those backups are governed by the terms of Apple or your backup provider.

Changes to This Policy

The Provider may change this policy as needed. The updated policy will be published on this site.
