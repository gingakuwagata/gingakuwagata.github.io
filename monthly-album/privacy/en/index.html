Monthly Album Privacy Policy

Original

Yuto Kuwabara (the "Provider") explains below how Monthly Album (the "App") handles information.

Personal Information

Personal information refers to information about an individual that can identify a specific person through details such as name, date of birth, phone number, email address, or other descriptions contained therein (including information that can be easily combined with other information to identify a specific individual).

Information Handled by the App

  • With permission to access the photo library, the App displays selected photos and videos and uses them to create calendar images. Photos, videos, notes, and selections are stored on your device and are not uploaded to the Provider's servers. If you choose to share content, it is sent to the destination you select.
  • Firebase Authentication creates an identifier for anonymous sign-in. No name or email address is required. The identifier is used to check paid-feature access stored in Firestore.
  • Firebase Analytics records usage such as screen views, feature use, the number and type of photos or videos selected, and purchase-related actions. Analytics events do not contain photo or video content or note text.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash information, device and OS information, app version, and identifiers. Firebase Remote Config processes technical information needed to deliver app settings.
  • If you contact us, we receive the information you provide. Apple processes in-app purchases and payments.

Purposes and Service Providers

We use this information to provide the App, verify purchase status, investigate failures, analyze usage, improve features, and respond to inquiries. Google LLC processes information needed to provide Firebase services, and Apple Inc. processes information needed for in-app purchases. We do not disclose user information to other third parties for these purposes except as required by law.

Storage and Deletion

You can remove locally stored photo selections and notes in the App or by deleting the App. Contact us below to request access, correction, or deletion of information controlled by the Provider. Retention by external services follows their respective policies.

Management and Security of Personal Information

The Provider maintains the accuracy of personal information and manages it securely. The Provider implements appropriate information security measures to prevent the loss, destruction, falsification, or leakage of personal information.

Amendments to This Privacy Policy

The Provider will periodically review the management of personal information, strive for continuous improvement, and may revise this Policy as necessary. Any revised Privacy Policy will be published on this site as needed.

Procedures for Disclosure of Personal Information

The App will respond without delay to requests from individuals regarding notification of the purpose of use, disclosure, correction, addition or deletion of content, suspension of use, erasure, or suspension of third-party provision of their personal information (hereinafter "Disclosure Requests"). For details, please contact us at the address below.
